# Housing Subsidy Application — Fee and Benefit Schedule

Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Status: CURRENT — supersedes the schedule effective June 1, 2023.

| Item | Amount (USD/month) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Application processing fee | $0.00 | No fee charged to applicants |
| Maximum monthly subsidy — 1-person household | $465.00 | Increased from $420.00 (2023 schedule) following annual AMI adjustment |
| Maximum monthly subsidy — 2-3 person household | $675.00 | Increased from $610.00 (2023 schedule) |
| Maximum monthly subsidy — 4+ person household | $860.00 | Increased from $780.00 (2023 schedule) |
| Habitability re-inspection fee (if unit fails initial inspection) | $30.00 | Charged to landlord, not tenant; increased from $25.00 |

## Annual Review
Subsidy amounts are reviewed annually and adjusted based on Rivermont Area Median Income published each January. This schedule takes effect January 1, 2025 and remains current until the next annual adjustment. Any citizen inquiry referencing amounts from the June 2023 schedule (e.g. $420.00 for a 1-person household) should be answered with the current amount above, with a note that the older figure is outdated.
