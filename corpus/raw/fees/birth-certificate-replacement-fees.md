# Birth Certificate Replacement — Fee Schedule

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

| Item | Fee (USD) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Standard certified copy (mail delivery, 10 business days) | $18.00 | Per copy |
| Expedited certified copy (in-person, same day) | $35.00 | Subject to counter availability |
| Additional copies requested in the same transaction | $12.00 | Per additional copy |
| Delayed Registration Affidavit processing fee | $45.00 | Separate service, see referral note in eligibility document |
| Fee waiver | $0.00 | Available only to applicants presenting a current means-tested benefits card issued by the State Department of Social Services |

## Payment Methods

Accepted: money order, debit/credit card at Registry counters, and online payment portal. Cash is not accepted at any Registry location as of this fee schedule's effective date.

## Refund Policy

Fees are non-refundable once processing has begun, except where the Registry is unable to locate the underlying registration record, in which case a full refund is issued within 15 business days.
