# Housing Subsidy Application — Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much subsidy can I get for a 2-person household?**
A: Refer to the current fee and benefit schedule for the applicable amount — the maximum monthly subsidy for a 2-3 person household changes periodically following the annual Area Median Income adjustment, so always confirm against the schedule currently in effect rather than a figure you may have seen previously.

**Q: I already get a federal rent subsidy — can I also get this one?**
A: No. Per the eligibility criteria, a household already receiving an equivalent rent subsidy from a state or federal program is not eligible for this municipal program concurrently.

**Q: I own a small vacation cabin outside the city — does that disqualify me?**
A: Generally yes, ownership of residential real property anywhere disqualifies a household under the eligibility criteria, unless that property is documented as uninhabitable by a certified inspector. This is a fact-specific determination and should be escalated for Officer review if there is any ambiguity.

**Q: How long does the whole process take?**
A: Assuming no escalation, roughly 20–27 business days from submission to your first subsidy payment, per the current procedure and timeline document.

**Q: What if my unit fails the habitability inspection?**
A: A re-inspection fee applies, charged to your landlord rather than to you — check the current fee schedule for the exact amount, as it is periodically adjusted.
