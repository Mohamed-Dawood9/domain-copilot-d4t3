# Birth Certificate Replacement — Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I request my adult child's birth certificate on their behalf?**
A: Not directly. Under the eligibility criteria, only the registrant themselves (if 18 or older), or someone holding a notarized power of attorney from the registrant, may apply. A parent's relationship alone does not establish eligibility once the registrant is an adult.

**Q: My birth was registered in a different city. Can Rivermont Civil Registry issue my certificate?**
A: No. Eligibility requires the original registration to exist in the Rivermont Civil Registry database. You would need to apply through the registry of the jurisdiction where your birth was originally registered.

**Q: I lost my ID. Can I still apply?**
A: You must present one primary and one secondary form of identification as described in the eligibility criteria. Without a primary government-issued photo ID, the Registry cannot verify your identity, and your application cannot proceed until you can present acceptable alternative documentation — contact the Registry directly to discuss options.

**Q: How much does it cost to get a certified copy quickly?**
A: The expedited, same-day, in-person service is $35.00 per copy, subject to counter availability on the day of your visit, as set out in the current fee schedule.

**Q: What if I can't afford the fee?**
A: A fee waiver is available if you present a current means-tested benefits card issued by the State Department of Social Services, as described in the fee schedule.

**Q: Is there a fee if the Registry can't find my record?**
A: No. If the Registry is unable to locate the underlying registration record, any fee already paid is fully refunded within 15 business days.
