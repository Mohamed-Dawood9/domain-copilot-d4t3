# Birth Certificate Replacement — Eligibility Criteria

## 1. Purpose

This document sets out the conditions under which a resident of the City of Rivermont may apply for a replacement or certified copy of a birth certificate originally issued by the Rivermont Civil Registry.

## 2. Eligible Applicants

2.1. The person named on the birth certificate ("the registrant"), if aged 18 or older.

2.2. A parent or legal guardian of the registrant, if the registrant is a minor (under 18 years of age).

2.3. A legal representative holding a notarized power of attorney explicitly authorizing the request of civil documents on behalf of the registrant.

2.4. The spouse of the registrant, upon presentation of a valid marriage certificate, for the sole purpose of joint property or inheritance filings.

2.5. A legally appointed executor of the registrant's estate, where the registrant is deceased, upon presentation of a grant of probate or equivalent court order.

## 3. Ineligible Applicants

3.1. Any third party without one of the relationships described in Section 2, including employers, landlords, or unrelated individuals, regardless of stated purpose.

3.2. A former spouse, unless the request is made pursuant to a court order specifically compelling the Registry to release the document.

## 4. Required Original Registration

4.1. Eligibility under this document applies only where the original birth record exists in the Rivermont Civil Registry database. Births registered in another jurisdiction are not eligible for replacement through this service; the applicant must be directed to the jurisdiction of original registration.

4.2. Where the original registration record cannot be located due to registry digitization gaps (records prior to 1985 in particular), the applicant is eligible to apply for a Delayed Registration Affidavit process instead, which is a separate service.

## 5. Identity Verification Requirement

5.1. All applicants must present one primary form of government-issued photo identification and one secondary form of identification (e.g. utility bill, bank statement) matching the applicant's name and current address on file.

5.2. Where the applicant's name has changed since the original registration (e.g. through marriage or legal name change), a certified copy of the name-change instrument must be provided.

## 6. Ambiguous or Contested Cases

6.1. Where eligibility cannot be clearly established from Sections 2–5 — including disputed guardianship, unclear legal representative authority, or an applicant claiming an exception not explicitly listed above — front-line staff and any automated system MUST escalate the case to a Registry Officer for manual review rather than approving or denying the request. No automated determination of eligibility is final in a contested case.

6.2. Staff and systems must not infer eligibility from circumstantial evidence (e.g. shared surname, claimed family relationship without documentation). Eligibility is established only by the documentary evidence described in this section.
