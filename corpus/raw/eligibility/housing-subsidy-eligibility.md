# Housing Subsidy Application — Eligibility Criteria

## 1. Purpose
This document sets out eligibility for the Rivermont Rental Housing Subsidy Program, which provides monthly rent assistance to qualifying low-income households.

## 2. Household Income Threshold
2.1. Applicant household gross annual income must not exceed 60% of the Rivermont Area Median Income (AMI), as published annually by the Housing Authority.

2.2. Household income includes wages, self-employment income, pension income, and government benefits, but excludes one-time gifts and tax refunds.

## 3. Residency Requirement
3.1. At least one adult household member must have resided within Rivermont city limits for a minimum of 6 continuous months immediately preceding application.

## 4. Household Composition
4.1. The subsidy applies per household, defined as persons sharing a single lease or rental agreement and a common kitchen.

4.2. A household already receiving an equivalent rent subsidy from a state or federal program is not eligible for this municipal program concurrently, to prevent duplicate benefit ("double-dipping").

## 5. Property Requirements
5.1. The rented unit must be located within Rivermont city limits and be the applicant's primary residence.

5.2. The unit must pass a basic habitability inspection conducted by the Housing Authority within 30 days of application approval.

## 6. Disqualifying Conditions
6.1. Any household with an outstanding eviction judgment against a member within the past 24 months is ineligible, unless the judgment has been vacated or satisfied.

6.2. Ownership of residential real property by any household member, in Rivermont or elsewhere, disqualifies the household, except where the owned property is uninhabitable and documented as such by a certified inspector.

## 7. Ambiguous Cases
7.1. Where household composition, income calculation, or property ownership status cannot be clearly determined from submitted documentation, the case must be escalated to a Housing Authority Officer for manual determination. No automated or preliminary approval is to be communicated to the applicant as final in such cases.
