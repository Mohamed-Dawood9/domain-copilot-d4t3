# Municipal Regulation: Small Business License

## Chapter 1: General Provisions

**Section 1.1: Title and Authority**
This regulation shall be known as the Municipal Regulation: Small Business License. It is enacted pursuant to the authority granted by the Municipal Government Act to regulate and govern matters related to small business license.

**Section 1.2: Purpose**
The purpose of this regulation is to establish a comprehensive framework for small business license. It aims to ensure fairness, efficiency, and compliance with all applicable state and federal laws.

**Section 1.3: Definitions**
For the purposes of this regulation, the following definitions apply:
1. **Applicant**: Any individual, business, or entity that submits an application for small business license.
2. **Department**: The Municipal Department of Administrative Services.
3. **Officer**: A duly authorized municipal employee responsible for reviewing and approving applications.
4. **Eligible Entity**: A person or organization that meets all criteria established in Chapter 2.

## Chapter 2: Eligibility Requirements

**Section 2.1: General Eligibility**
To be eligible, an Applicant must:
1. Be a resident of the municipality for no less than 12 consecutive months.
2. Be in good standing with all municipal tax authorities.
3. Submit a complete application form with all required supporting documentation.

**Section 2.2: Specific Criteria**
In addition to the general eligibility, the following specific criteria must be met:
- For individuals: Proof of identity and residency.
- For businesses: A valid commercial registration and tax clearance certificate.

## Chapter 3: Application Procedures

**Section 3.1: Submission**
Applications must be submitted through the official municipal portal or in person at the Department office. Mailed applications are no longer accepted as of the effective date of this regulation.

**Section 3.2: Review Process**
The Officer shall review the application within 14 business days. If the application is incomplete, the Officer shall notify the Applicant, who will have 30 days to provide the missing information.

## Chapter 4: Fees and Financial Provisions

**Section 4.1: Standard Fees**
All applications are subject to a non-refundable processing fee, as detailed in the official Municipal Fee Schedule. Fees must be paid at the time of submission.

**Section 4.2: Fee Waivers**
Applicants demonstrating extreme financial hardship may apply for a fee waiver. The waiver application must include the last two years of tax returns and a sworn affidavit of financial status.

## Chapter 5: Appeals and Grievances

**Section 5.1: Right to Appeal**
Any Applicant whose application is denied has the right to appeal the decision.

**Section 5.2: Appeal Procedure**
Appeals must be filed in writing with the Municipal Appeals Board within 30 days of the denial notice. The Board shall schedule a hearing within 45 days of receiving the appeal.

## Chapter 6: Enforcement and Penalties

**Section 6.1: Violations**
Providing false information on an application or failing to comply with the terms of an approved application constitutes a violation of this regulation.

**Section 6.2: Penalties**
Violators may be subject to fines up to $5,000, revocation of the approved status, and possible criminal prosecution for fraud.
