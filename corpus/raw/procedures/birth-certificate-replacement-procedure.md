# Birth Certificate Replacement — Procedure and Timeline

## Step 1: Submit Application
Applicant submits Form CR-14 (Civil Registry Replacement Request) either in person at any Rivermont Civil Registry counter or via the online citizen portal. Processing clock starts upon submission with all required identity documents attached (see eligibility document Section 5).

Estimated duration: same day (submission only).

## Step 2: Identity and Eligibility Verification
A Registry clerk verifies the applicant's identification and eligibility category against the criteria in the eligibility document. Where the case is ambiguous or contested per eligibility Section 6, the clerk escalates to a Registry Officer.

Estimated duration: 1–3 business days for standard cases; contested cases have no fixed timeline pending Officer review.

## Step 3: Record Lookup
The Registry database is searched for the original birth record using the registrant's full name, date of birth, and parents' names if provided. If the record predates 1985 and is not found in the digitized database, the applicant is referred to the Delayed Registration Affidavit process (a separate service, not covered by this procedure).

Estimated duration: 1–2 business days.

## Step 4: Certificate Issuance
Upon successful verification and record lookup, a certified copy is printed, sealed, and either mailed to the applicant's address on file or made available for in-person collection, depending on the service tier selected (standard vs. expedited, per the fee schedule).

Estimated duration: standard tier 5–7 business days after Step 3 completes; expedited tier same business day, subject to counter availability.

## Total Estimated Timeline
Standard tier: 7–10 business days from submission to receipt.
Expedited tier: same day, where all prior steps are completed without escalation.

## Escalation Path
Any case flagged under eligibility Section 6 (ambiguous or contested) pauses the timeline above until a Registry Officer issues a manual determination. No estimated timeline applies to escalated cases; the Officer's office will contact the applicant directly.
