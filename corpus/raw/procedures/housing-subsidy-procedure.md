# Housing Subsidy Application — Procedure and Timeline

## Step 1: Submit Application
Applicant submits Form HA-07 with income documentation (pay stubs or benefit statements for the preceding 3 months), proof of residency (lease agreement), and household composition declaration.

Estimated duration: same day (submission only).

## Step 2: Income and Residency Verification
Housing Authority staff verify income against the current AMI threshold (see current fee/benefit schedule for annual figures) and residency duration.

Estimated duration: 5–7 business days.

## Step 3: Property Habitability Inspection
An inspector visits the rental unit to confirm basic habitability standards are met.

Estimated duration: 10–15 business days from Step 2 completion, subject to inspector availability.

## Step 4: Determination and Enrollment
Upon successful verification and inspection, the household is enrolled and the first subsidy payment is issued to the landlord on the household's behalf, at the amount specified in the current fee/benefit schedule for the applicable household size.

Estimated duration: 5 business days after Step 3 completes.

## Total Estimated Timeline
20–27 business days from submission to first payment, assuming no escalation.

## Escalation Path
Cases flagged under eligibility Section 7 (ambiguous composition, income, or ownership status) are paused pending Housing Authority Officer review with no fixed timeline.
